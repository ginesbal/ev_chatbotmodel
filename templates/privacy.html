Privacy

EVison Advisor is a portfolio project built by Ehrl Balquin to demonstrate a search interface for electric vehicles. It is not a commercial service and does not sell anything.

What we collect

  • A session cookie used to keep your shortlist and theme preference between page loads.
  • Standard server logs (IP address, user agent, request path) retained briefly for debugging.

What we do not collect

  • No account registration, no email, no phone number, no payment details.
  • No third-party analytics trackers or advertising pixels.

Third parties

Fonts, icons, and the stylesheet CDN are loaded from public providers (Google Fonts, jsDelivr, Font Awesome, Cloudflare). Those providers may observe request metadata per their own policies.
